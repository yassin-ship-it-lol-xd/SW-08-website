Case study: Hey Sid

How I built the sales engine at Hey Sid

Hey Sid is a Gothenburg-based B2B growth company with customers in the Nordics, the UK and the US. I built and ran the engine behind their own pipeline.

74meetings in 11 weeks
92%show rate, up from 75%
21meetings in 6 weeks from the new SDR channel
2xreply rate with signal-based outreach

01

The challenge

Scale outbound across cold email, LinkedIn and cold calling: more volume and more efficiency, without losing quality. Add an SDR call channel. And make it all work for the account executives who close the deals, with systems and procedures that run every day.

02

The channels

Three channels, one engine. Every touch tracked in one place.

Cold email

Signal-based and contextual

Smaller, targeted campaigns built around a verified reason to talk now, instead of big generic sends.

LinkedIn

From the sales team's own profiles

Outreach and follow-up from the people who would take the meeting, so every conversation started with a real name.

Cold calling

An SDR dialling from ready lists

Warm leads that had gone quiet turned into call lists with the full context, so the SDR could just dial.

03

How it worked with the sales team

The engine only counts if the people who sell get more out of their week.

Account executives

Walked into held meetings, prepared

First meetings were booked, confirmed and followed up until the prospect showed. The AE got the context before the call and spent the time selling.

SDR

Dialled instead of building lists

Ready call lists, conversation history and call tasks in the CRM. Less admin, more conversations.

Sales leadership

Numbers they could trust

Booked vs held meetings and show rate tracked the same way every month, plus a weekly report on every active campaign.

04

What I built

  • One source of truth for target accounts and contacts, so every list started from the same clean data
  • Signal-based and contextual campaigns across cold email and LinkedIn
  • A new SDR call channel, fed with ready call lists built from warm leads
  • The daily sales operations: reply handling, follow-up tracking, and a do-not-contact list kept up to date every week
  • Appointment setting from first reply to held meeting, with a daily check on every booked meeting
  • AI tooling and automations that took the admin off the team

06

AI agents that did the admin

No chasing invites, no manual list building, no guessing which meetings happened.

Show-up agent

Every weekday morning it checks the reps' calendars and the CRM, flags invites that haven't been accepted, drafts reminders and call tasks, and logs every touch in Slack.

Meeting outcome check

Once a month each rep gets a Slack message for every first meeting. One click marks it held, no-show or cancelled, and it's written back to HubSpot.

Call list builder

Turns warm leads that went quiet into ready call lists for the SDR, with the conversation context and a call task in the CRM.

Closed-won to LinkedIn

When a deal closes, connection requests go out to the new customer's team from the founder's profile, after an approval in Slack.

Campaign health report

A weekly report on every active campaign, so nothing runs dry or breaks without someone knowing.

Do-not-contact sync

Customers, open deals and opt-outs are kept out of every list automatically, refreshed every week.
