The two situations I see most, and what I do in each.

Scenario 1

Your sellers book their own meetings

The same people prospect, follow up and close, so pipeline goes up and down with how busy they are.

What I do

I take care of the pipeline end to end. I build and run cold email and LinkedIn outreach, book the meetings, and hand them to your sellers.

Scenario 2

You have SDRs, but the results aren't there

They spend their time building lists, looking up numbers and doing admin instead of dialling.

What I do

I make the whole team more efficient. Your SDRs dial from ready lists with the right people and the right numbers. Your AEs spend more time with prospects. I run outbound on top: the opportunities go to your SDRs, and they send back the people who need a follow-up later.

Either way, it all works together as one sales engine, not as separate campaigns.

This is for you if

  • You sell B2B and have salespeople (sellers, account executives or SDRs) who can take meetings
  • You do roughly €1M to €6M a year in revenue
  • Your pipeline depends on how much time your sellers have to prospect
  • Your SDRs spend more time building lists than dialling
  • You want to add a new sales channel, like cold email or LinkedIn, without hiring for it

How it works

STEP 1

Map the market

I map your total addressable market: every account that fits, and the best people to talk to at each one.

STEP 2

Find the right timing

Where there's a buying signal, we lead with it. Where there isn't, the outreach is highly relevant and contextual, with a strong offer. Never generic.

STEP 3

Reach out and follow up across channels

I run cold email and LinkedIn, and your SDRs call from ready lists. Every reply is tracked. If someone says "talk to me in three months", that follow-up happens in three months.

STEP 4

From first reply to held meeting

From the first positive reply to the first meeting, I own the prospect. If they go quiet before booking, I follow up until they book. Once they book, I follow up until they show up. Then your seller gets the full context: why this account, why now, who decides.

The setup depends on your team. The starting point is always the same: the right market, the right people, the right timing.
